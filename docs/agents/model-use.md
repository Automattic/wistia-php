# Auxiliary model-use policy

## Approved mode: none

The maintainer approved no auxiliary assessment use during repository-setup discovery, reaffirmed by the approved cumulative setup plan.

This applies to `ask-jev`, `ask-nimble`, `ask-clef`, `ask-clef-flash`, and similar auxiliary assessment services:

- Zero authorized requests, retries, or spending.
- No assessment-service startup, substitution, or evidence disclosure to those services.
- No installation, download, or credential changes follow from this policy.
- Ordinary engineering is the fallback. If evidence is insufficient, report the bounded gap rather than consulting an assessment service.

The restriction applies project-wide, including delegated work and resumed sessions; resumption creates no allowance. Per-model budgets, endpoints, and lifecycle grants are not applicable because no assessment use is authorized. The rationale is to perform the work with ordinary engineering without optional assessment services.

This policy does not prohibit configured coding agents, bounded delegation, independent reviewer agents, or lead-owned repairs. Their authority and limits are in [workflow](workflow.md).

## Changes

Installation or availability of a skill does not grant permission. A future assessment request must first resolve this prohibition with the maintainer through an exact approved policy change. Record the identity, purpose, disclosure, finite shared limits, lifecycle, fallback, and approval provenance before any newly allowed route is used. Preserve existing provenance and consumed allowances on subsequent changes; unknown permission remains ungranted.

The lead owns policy reconciliation and pointer upkeep. Assessment packages own their runtime checks; publication of this policy does not demonstrate their enforcement.
